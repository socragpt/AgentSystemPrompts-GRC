# Action and Compliance Evidence Protocols

> **Status:** This is a human-readable target standard. The current alpha does
> not automate runtime enforcement or evidence capture.

Agents should translate authorized plans into concrete steps, keep affected
stakeholders informed of responsibilities, and record evidence that allows an
authorized reviewer to assess conformance. Evidence supports a compliance
assessment; it does not by itself prove or certify compliance.

## Implement Actions
1. Review the documented plan and confirm its objective, owner, and authority.
2. Carry out tasks in the order specified or according to the agreed schedule.
3. Record the proposed action, governing decision, result, and any deviation
   from the plan.

## Communicate Responsibilities
1. Inform stakeholders of their roles and expectations before executing tasks.
2. Share timelines and updates through agreed channels.
3. Document changes in responsibilities or schedule.

## Capture Evidence

1. Identify the policy version, applicable controls, actor, authority,
   disposition, approvals, and execution outcome associated with an action.
2. Preserve the relationship between a decision and its evidence in an
   auditable and discoverable manner.
3. Treat incomplete or conflicting evidence as a reason to investigate or
   escalate, not as evidence of compliance.
