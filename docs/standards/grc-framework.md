# Governance, Risk, and Compliance Framework

This document summarizes the core concepts of Governance, Risk, and Compliance
(GRC). It explains how these concepts apply to AI agent coordination.

> **Status:** This document provides design guidance for the target governance
> harness. It does not describe runtime controls implemented by the current
> alpha.

## Governance

Governance defines the structure for decision-making and accountability. For
AI agents, governance defines how agents interact with human stakeholders and
with one another.

### Key Points
1. **Strategic Alignment** – Ensure AI agent goals align with the organization's mission and values.
2. **Decision Authority** – Clarify which roles can initiate or approve actions.
3. **Oversight** – Maintain visibility into agent activities through reports and audits.

## Risk Management

Risk management identifies and mitigates uncertainties that could affect
objectives. AI agents must be aware of operational, legal, and ethical risks.

### Key Points
1. **Risk Identification** – Agents document and share potential threats to achieving goals.
2. **Mitigation Plans** – Agents implement controls or contingency plans for identified risks.
3. **Continuous Monitoring** – Agents track risk indicators and escalate issues when thresholds are exceeded.

## Compliance

Compliance requires adherence to policies, standards, and external regulations.
Under this target framework, AI agents follow documented procedures and
generate evidence of conformance.

### Key Points
1. **Policy Adherence** – Agents reference current policies when making decisions.
2. **Record Keeping** – Decisions, actions, and results are logged so authorized
   reviewers can assess conformance.
3. **Audit Support** – Agents provide data required for internal or external audits.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Roles identify who performs tasks. Responsibilities define what each role must
achieve. A clear mapping supports coordinated AI behavior.

| Role | Responsibility | Example Agent Task |
|------|----------------|--------------------|
| **Governance Lead** | Set strategic objectives and approve major actions. | Review and authorize deployment plans generated by agents. |
| **Risk Manager** | Identify and evaluate risks; assign mitigation strategies. | Analyze risk reports from agents and update controls. |
| **Compliance Officer** | Ensure adherence to policies and regulations. | Verify agent-produced logs meet reporting requirements. |
| **Operations Coordinator** | Execute approved plans and monitor performance. | Orchestrate agent tasks and track progress toward goals. |

## Mapping to AI Agent Coordination

1. **Clear Hierarchy** – Agents respect the decision boundaries that Governance defines.
2. **Shared Risk Awareness** – Agents exchange risk data and align on mitigation steps.
3. **Policy Decision Points** – The target governance harness evaluates
   proposed actions against applicable policy before dispatch.
4. **Feedback Loops** – Governance leads and managers review agent outputs to refine strategies.

These are target design requirements, not claims about implemented runtime
behavior. If implemented and validated, they can help agents act more
consistently and transparently within organizational objectives.
