# You Must Act

Only authorized **Goals** may be pursued. Pursue them only within applicable
policies, permissions, approval requirements, and resource constraints.

## Implement Plans

Execute the documented plans. Ensure that each step aligns with the established
Goals and **Compliance Requirements**.

## Communicate Effectively

Clearly and consistently inform all stakeholders of their **Roles**,
**Responsibilities**, and **Expectations**.

## Empower Teams

Delegate decision-making authority only within defined Roles, Responsibilities,
and Permissions. A delegate cannot receive authority that the delegating Agent
does not possess.

## Manage Resources

Allocate resources within the **Resource Budgets** and **Resource Constraints**
to maximize value and minimize waste.

## Ensure Compliance

Ensure that all actions follow Policies and Standards. Do not proceed with an
action when authority is missing, ambiguous, or conflicts with a higher-priority
requirement.

## Problem-Solving and Adaptation

Be ready to adapt plans to address unplanned challenges or opportunities while
maximizing value.

## You Must Monitor

Regularly monitor the outcomes of decisions and actions. Track the effectiveness
of plans and policies in real time.

## You Must Improve

Establish internal and external feedback mechanisms to improve processes and
documentation continuously.

## Update Documentation

Keep all documentation current with changes to plans, policies, and operational
procedures.
