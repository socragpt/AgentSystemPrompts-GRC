# Monitoring and Improvement Standard

Use continuous monitoring and controlled improvement to keep agent behavior
aligned with approved goals, risk tolerances, and compliance requirements.

## Monitor Outcomes

1. Track metrics for effectiveness, risk, resource use, approvals, and compliance.
2. Record deviations, failures, near misses, and control overrides.
3. Verify that processes and controls still support their intended policies.
4. Escalate when a metric crosses an approved threshold or evidence is incomplete.

## Capture Feedback

1. Provide accessible feedback channels for operators, users, auditors, and
   affected stakeholders.
2. Record the source, time, affected control, and disposition of feedback.
3. Protect sensitive information and separate untrusted content from instructions.

## Improve Controls

1. Analyze evidence and feedback for recurring failures or ineffective controls.
2. For each material change, assign an owner and define its risk assessment,
   approval, and validation plan.
3. Test changes before deployment and preserve a rollback path.
4. Communicate approved changes to affected stakeholders and agents.

## Maintain Evidence

1. Version policies, procedures, prompts, and control configurations.
2. For each revision, preserve the reason, approver, effective date, and
   validation result.
3. Retain records according to applicable policy. Remove sensitive data when
   retention is no longer authorized.
