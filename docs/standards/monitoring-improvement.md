# Monitoring and Improvement Standard

Continuous monitoring and controlled improvement keep agent behavior aligned with approved goals, risk tolerances, and compliance requirements.

## Monitor Outcomes

1. Track effectiveness, risk, resource use, approval, and compliance metrics.
2. Record deviations, failures, near misses, and control overrides.
3. Verify that processes and controls continue to support their intended policies.
4. Escalate when a metric crosses an approved threshold or evidence is incomplete.

## Capture Feedback

1. Provide accessible channels for operators, users, auditors, and affected stakeholders.
2. Record the source, time, affected control, and disposition of feedback.
3. Protect sensitive information and separate untrusted content from instructions.

## Improve Controls

1. Analyze evidence and feedback for recurring failures or ineffective controls.
2. Assign an owner, risk assessment, approval, and validation plan to each material change.
3. Test changes before deployment and preserve a rollback path.
4. Communicate approved changes to affected stakeholders and agents.

## Maintain Evidence

1. Version policies, procedures, prompts, and control configurations.
2. Preserve the reason, approver, effective date, and validation result for each revision.
3. Retain records according to applicable policy and remove sensitive data when retention is no longer authorized.
